Everything you need to know about the inaugural edition of SORA Youth Summit 2026.

SORA Youth Summit is a professional platform for diplomacy, negotiation, and informed debate. It brings together young leaders to engage with contemporary global and national issues in a structured conference format.

The summit will be held on 3rd – 4th October 2026 at Father Agnel College, Vashi, Navi Mumbai.

The summit is open to individual delegates as well as institutional delegations. Young leaders interested in diplomacy, debate, and leadership are welcome to apply.

Delegate applications are currently open. You can register as an Individual Delegate or through an Institutional Delegation using the official registration forms shared by the Secretariat.

Early Bird registration is available for a limited period.

Guided by the principles of Vox · Vision · Veritas, the summit encourages participants to discover new ideas, connect with young minds, and lead with purpose.
